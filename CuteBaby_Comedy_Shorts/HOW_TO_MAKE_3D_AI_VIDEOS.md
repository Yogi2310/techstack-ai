Hedra creates true video where the 3D baby speaks the dialogue with lifelike facial motion:
1. Go to [hedra.com](https://www.hedra.com) (free account).
2. Click **Create Character Video**.
3. Upload your character image: `D:\Antigravity\Pro\CuteBaby_Comedy_Shorts\characters\cute_ai_baby_golgappa.jpg`.
